Refuge LARP

A high-fantasy, player-versus-environment LARP played in person across the US and Canada. Players take on a character in a shared world of many Realms, facing adventures together and fighting with foam weapons. Local chapters host weekend events and single-night tavern games, all part of one ongoing story.

How to start

Play a character, follow a shared story, and face adventures with other players. Combat is fought with foam weapons.

  1. Choose a chapter.Use the directory to find a community near you.
  2. Ask about your first event.Confirm what to bring, the setting, and how to prepare.
